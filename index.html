1. About

Across biology and AI, I work on the same problem: turning messy, incomplete evidence into conclusions and systems people can trust.

During my neuroscience PhD, I discovered cell-type-specific alternative splicing across more than 100,000 single cells, then validated the result experimentally through mouse genetics, blood-brain-barrier assays, and confocal imaging. That work became a first-author paper in the Journal of Neuroscience.

My recent AI work applies the same rigor to model behavior and scientific software. I identified and experimentally reproduced a class of silent failure modes in data-science agents across 1,000 primary runs1; built SC-Referee, a deterministic scientific-analysis checker delivered as a Python application and Claude Code skill2; and shipped systems for Arena.ai prediction, agentic research, embedding-based retrieval, fact-checking, and automated publishing.

I work end to end: framing the question, gathering evidence, building the system, designing the evaluation, tracing failures, and communicating what the result does and does not support. I am most useful where technical building and judgment meet—evaluating AI systems, designing reliable workflows, or turning difficult scientific data into decisions.

2. Projects

Silent Precondition Traps in Data-Science Agents

Identified and experimentally reproduced a class of agent failures in which a familiar analysis tool silently violates an uninspected data precondition, producing a confident and repeatable wrong answer. Designed two frozen benchmark batteries across 10 OpenAI and Anthropic model-scaffold configurations and 1,000 primary runs; a short data-hygiene intervention reduced failures roughly fourfold but did not eliminate them. Completed as an independent study with public code, manuscript, and permanently archived artifacts.

SC-Referee: Deterministic Scientific-Analysis Checker

Built for Anthropic's Built with Claude: Life Sciences Builder track, SC-Referee is a deterministic checker for single-cell analysis folders delivered as a Python application and Claude Code skill. It reconstructs experimental designs, asks scientists to confirm ambiguous context, and dispatches 12+ verifiers for pseudoreplication, confounding, multiple testing, model-data mismatch, aggregation errors, and circular analysis. Evidence-linked reports mark each conclusion clear, flagged, needs review, or not evaluated.

Project Soothsayer: LLM Evaluation & Prediction System

Predicts Arena.ai human-preference scores from benchmark data alone despite only 138 labeled models, 122 features, and ~40% missing data. Custom per-cell imputation, four original LLM benchmarks, PLS supervision, and adaptive local regression reduced RMSE 29% versus the best regularized baseline. A fully refit temporal walk-forward achieved R² = 0.937.

Newscaster: Agentic AI Research & Automated Publishing

Built and operate a daily multi-provider system that discovers stories across seven sources, uses a LangGraph controller to pursue and validate evidence, demands counter-evidence, fact-checks scripts, synthesizes speech, and publishes to YouTube — 525+ episodes. Its embedding-based RAG memory retrieves relevant reporting from earlier episodes; the right prior coverage ranked first in 12/12 recurring-story tests, with 93% of the top six results relevant.

PhD Research: Adgrl2 Alternative Splicing in Cerebrovasculature

Discovered that Adgrl2 uses cell-type-specific alternative splicing to serve distinct roles in neurons (synapse assembly) versus endothelial cells (blood-brain barrier integrity). Built end-to-end R/Python pipelines for single-cell RNA-seq analysis across 100K+ cells, then validated computationally identified splicing patterns at the bench through conditional knockouts, isoform swap experiments, BBB permeability assays, and confocal imaging.

Polygenic Disease Gene Prediction

Built multi-omic networks (~65K nodes, ~7.3M edges) linking DNA, RNA, and protein layers to predict how disease-associated genes converge on common processes. Graph-based semi-supervised learning traced GWAS risk loci to microglial activation and synaptic signaling. Co-first-author paper in BMC Bioinformatics.
