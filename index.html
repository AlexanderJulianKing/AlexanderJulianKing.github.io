01LLM evaluation

Project Soothsayer: LLM Evaluation & Prediction System

A complete system for predicting how people will rate a language model on Arena.ai. It runs four benchmarks I designed (emotional intelligence, creative writing, commonsense logic, and writing style) on every model. It fills the gaps in the benchmark data with a custom per-cell imputer. Then adaptive local regression predicts each model's human preference score. The full system’s RMSE is 29% lower than the best regularized baseline’s, and its R² in a fully refit temporal walk-forward is 0.937.

02Agentic systems

Newscaster: An Automated Daily News Show

I built a daily news show that is produced from start to finish without a person in the loop. Every morning it scours the internet and picks the most important stories. Each lead story runs through a research loop I designed myself, complete with multiple adversarial fact checkers. 525+ episodes have been published so far.

03PhD research

Adgrl2 Alternative Splicing in Cerebrovasculature

I discovered that Adgrl2 is alternatively spliced in a cell-type-specific way, and that its isoforms have distinct roles in neurons (synapse assembly) and endothelial cells (blood-brain barrier integrity). To find this, I built end-to-end R/Python pipelines and analyzed single-cell RNA-seq data from 100K+ cells. I then validated the splicing patterns at the bench with conditional knockouts, isoform swap experiments, BBB permeability assays, and confocal imaging.

04Network biology

Polygenic Disease Gene Prediction

I built multi-omic networks (~65K nodes, ~7.3M edges) by linking DNA, RNA, and protein layers and their interaction networks, to predict how disease-associated genes converge on common processes. Using graph-based semi-supervised learning, I traced GWAS risk loci to microglial activation and synaptic signaling. I am co-first author of a paper on this approach in BMC Bioinformatics.
